Keyword guard

A conservative classifier rejects anything that isn't a plain read before it ever reaches the database.

Every query runs inside BEGIN READ ONLY, START TRANSACTION READ ONLY, or PRAGMA query_only.

Opt-in per connection

Only profiles you explicitly mark are visible to MCP. Everything else stays invisible to the assistant.

Is Rowhouse still open source?

Yes. The client — every feature in Free — stays MIT-licensed on GitHub. You can build it yourself and use it commercially.

Why pay, then?

Pro and Team pay for the extras (sync, schedules, team workspaces, policies) and for signed builds with auto-update. They also keep the free client maintained.

Does my data go through your servers?

No. Queries run directly from your machine, and sync never touches a Rowhouse server: it writes one file, encrypted with your passphrase, into a folder you already sync. Passwords are only included if you opt in.

What happens after a year of Pro updates?

Your license keeps working forever with the last version you got. Renew whenever you want the newer releases.
